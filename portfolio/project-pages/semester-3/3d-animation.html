Crypt Environment

Date Completed: December 2022

Description

A crypt environment modeled in Autodesk Maya experienced through Unity game engine.
This was the final project for my 3D modeling class.
The environment and props were all created by me in Autodesk Maya, and textured in Adobe Substance Painter.

The character controller was found on the Unity Asset Store HERE
