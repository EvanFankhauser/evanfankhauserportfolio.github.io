Shark Head Model

Date Completed: April 2025

Description

The second project for my 3D Asset Pipeline course.
This project required creating an animatronic animal head in the style of Five Nights at Freddy's.
The hammerhead shark head was modeled by me in Autodesk Maya, the body and skeleton were provided by the professor.
UV unwrapping was completed in Maya and textures were created in Adobe Substance Painter.
After completing the head, it was attached to the body, rigged, skinned, and exported to Unreal Engine 5.
In Unreal Engine, the model was pasted over the default character controller.
