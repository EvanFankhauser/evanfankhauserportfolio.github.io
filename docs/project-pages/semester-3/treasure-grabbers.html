Treasure Grabbers

Completed: December 2023

Description

Treasure Grabbers is a simulation focused around flocking behaviors.
There are three groups of creatures, each attempting to gather coins to
bring to their respective hoards. The creatures behave as follows:

    A Dragon that:
  • Wanders around the area, picking up gold as it comes into contact with it.
  • Returns to its hoard to deposit the gold once it has collected 25 gold.
  • Begins wandering to collect gold, repeating the cycle.
    Goblins who:
  • Pathfind to the nearest piece of gold, changing target as new gold appears.
  • Separate from other Goblins.
  • Avoid boulders and other creatures' hoard.
  • Flee from the dragon or cyclopes, even if it costs them gold.
  • Return to their hoard and deposit their gold once they have 5, then restarting the hunt.
    and Cyclopes that:
  • Pathfind to the nearest gold, not changing target until it is collected.
  • Separate from other cyclopes.
  • Avoid boulders.
  • Ignore the dragon and goblins.
  • Return to their hoard to deposit gold once they have collected 15, then restarting the hunt.
