Unreal Engine Shooting Gallery

Date Completed: December 2024

Description

As the final project for my level design course, I was tasked with making a shooting gallery in Unreal Engine 4.
The project uses the Unreal Engine First-Person Shooter template for the character and weapon.
The targets and doors were programed by me with Unreal Blueprints.
The project was divided into three phases: planning, building and reflecting.
The planning phase consisted of drafting diagrams for the level and a write-up to explain the thought process behind the plans.
The build phase consisted of creating the level, target and door functionality, and modeling the assets.
The reflection phase consisted of an in-class playtest and a written relection detailing player critiques and level quality.

The level is a shooting range designed to serve as a tutorial for a larger game.
The player is given a weapon to destroy targets. Destroying all targets on a floor opens a door to the next one.
The targets have a few different behaviors: moving side to side, swinging like a pendulem and popping up out of the floor.
They layout is designed to slowly introduce these behaviors to the player as a form of onboarding.
The first floor uses the simpler moving and swinging targets, and the popping ones are introduced
early on the second floor. The popping targets also have baseplates to indicate where they are, even
when not currently visible.
The targets and environments were modled by me in Autodesk Maya and textured in Adobe Substance Painter.
