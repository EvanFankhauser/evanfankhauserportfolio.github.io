Gridcast

Date Completed: December 2024

Description

A magic card battle strategy game made in Unity game engine as a group project.
Players take control of mages battling on a grid, casting spells to reduce their opponent's health to zero.
On their turn, players draw cards and play them onto the grid, where their magic power is unleashed!
More powerful spells require other cards to be discarded before activating.
Each turn, effects resolve, like projectiles moving across the board, or detonations counting down.

Gridcast is based on an analog game of the same name dreamt up by Joana Leong.
The original concept was strong, but players found all the parts difficult to track.
Digitizing the game proved to enhance the foundation as the computer now handles spell activation.
The project operated on a schedule of 2 week sprints, with playtests at the end of each sprint.
The project ended with a postmortem presentation.

My main responsibilities included programming player movement, implementing art assets, and onboarding.
Additionally, I completed the bulk of the work on sprint reports and organizing playtest results.
